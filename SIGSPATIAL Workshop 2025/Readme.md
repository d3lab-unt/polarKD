# LLM-Enhanced Knowledge Discovery for Polar Data Science
Polar Data Science Workshop at SIGSPATIAL 2025

Authors: Ajith Kumar Dugyala, Harini Varanasi, Sahara Ali

## Abstract
Much of the knowledge about data sources, methods, and findings in polar science is locked within unstructured PDF publications, making it difficult to trace datasets, reproduce analyses, or integrate insights across disciplines. This lack of structured access to research outputs hampers the FAIR principles of findability, accessibility, interoperability, and reusability that are central to accelerating discoveries in polar informatics. To address this gap, we present a Polar Knowledge Discovery (PolarKD) toolkit that automatically extracts dataset references and constructs knowledge graphs from scientific papers. By transforming literature into structured, machine-interpretable knowledge, our approach enables researchers to rapidly identify relevant datasets, uncover connections among studies, and build a foundation for integrating geospatial AI and machine learning methods.
